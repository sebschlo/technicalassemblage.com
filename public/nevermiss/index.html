NeverMiss

A real alarm for every meeting. NeverMiss reads every calendar synced to your iPhone and rings full-screen before each one — through silent mode and Focus, until you dismiss it.

What it does

  • Real alarms, not notifications — built on AlarmKit, so each alarm fires from the system even if the app is closed, breaks through silent mode and Focus, and keeps ringing until you act.
  • Every calendar you already have — iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange: anything in the iPhone Calendar app. Nothing to connect or re-sync.
  • Two alarms per meeting — 30 minutes and 2 minutes before by default. Pick your own lead times, add a day-of alarm for all-day events, or set a single meeting to Always or Never.
  • One tap into the call — Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex and FaceTime links are detected and the alarm opens them directly.
  • Stays out of the way — declined invitations and hidden calendars are skipped, and quiet hours keep overnight meetings silent.
  • Private by design — no account, no servers. Your calendar never leaves the device.
